# AI assistance disclosure

AI assisted research navigation, software architecture, coding, debugging, testing, GIS processing, interface design and documentation. The human team selected the challenge, supplied research/assets and approved the development priorities.

Scientific evidence is credited to original NASA, USGS, IAU, institutional and peer-reviewed sources. AI-generated descriptions are not treated as evidence. No AI-generated Mars photography or invented local measurements are used. Routing explanations are deterministic summaries of computed routes, parameters and comparisons; visitors need no AI account or API key.

Automated numerical and browser tests are recorded as such. They do not constitute NASA endorsement, independent scientific peer review, ground-truth hazard validation or astronaut-safety certification.
