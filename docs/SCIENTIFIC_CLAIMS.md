# Scientific claims register

The machine-readable register is `data/claims.json`. It includes claim text, source IDs and URLs, evidence section, location, verification/access status and uncertainty. Source-linked is not a claim of peer review or verification of every sentence in the original supplied research.

- **mawrth-summary**: Clay-bearing layered terrain records ancient interactions between water and rock. [Evidence](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/clay-minerals-in-mawrth-vallis-region-of-mars/). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
- **oxia-summary**: A broad clay-bearing plain studied as a rover landing site and a record of ancient aqueous alteration. [Evidence](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/possible-future-mars-landing-site-in-oxia-planum/). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
- **valles-summary**: This vast canyon system exposes layered deposits; Melas Chasma provides a window into its sedimentary history. [Evidence](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/deposits-along-the-northern-wall-of-melas-chasma/). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
- **meridiani-summary**: Opportunity explored hematite-bearing sedimentary materials in this equatorial plain, investigating evidence of past water. [Evidence](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/meridiani-planum-2/). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
- **jezero-summary**: A preserved delta and crater-floor rocks make Jezero a key site for investigating ancient environments. Perseverance explores this region. [Evidence](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
- **gale-summary**: Curiosity studies the layered rocks of Mount Sharp to investigate the history of ancient Martian environments. [Evidence](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/a-guide-to-gale-crater/). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
- **nili-summary**: Orbital spectroscopy reveals carbonate-bearing materials and other mineral signatures that constrain the region’s alteration history. [Evidence](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/mineral-spectra-from-nili-fossae-mars/). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
- **eberswalde-summary**: Preserved branching channels and delta deposits record sediment transport into an ancient standing body of water. [Evidence](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/eberswalde-delta-3-d/). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
- **holden-summary**: A former rover landing-site candidate with alluvial fans, flood deposits and clay-bearing sedimentary materials. [Evidence](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/four-finalist-landing-site-candidates-for-mars-science-laboratory/). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
- **gusev-summary**: Spirit investigated altered rocks in Columbia Hills. Silica-rich deposits have been interpreted as evidence of hydrothermal processes. [Evidence](https://ares.jsc.nasa.gov/missions/mer/important-discoveries.html). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
- **aram-summary**: Fractured terrain and layered sulfate-bearing deposits record a complex history of geological and chemical change. [Evidence](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia25358-a-complex-geologic-history-of-aram-chaos/). source-linked; automated access incomplete.
- **eridania-summary**: Mineral deposits in this broad basin system have been interpreted as ancient hydrothermal seafloor deposits. This is an interpretation, not evidence of life. [Evidence](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms15978). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
- **sirenum-summary**: Chloride-bearing deposits in topographic depressions provide evidence relevant to ancient water accumulation and evaporation. [Evidence](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/bright-exposures-of-chloride-salt-on-southern-mars/). source-linked; automated retrieval completed.
